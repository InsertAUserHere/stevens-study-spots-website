Campus Spots · Student prototype

Find your study spot, faster.

A campus app that shows which study spaces are free, lets you reserve a classroom, and gets you into a building after hours, so you spend less time walking around looking for a seat.

The problem

Study space on campus is limited, and students usually have to walk from building to building to find out what’s open. That’s hardest for commuters, who may only have a short gap between classes.

01

No visibility

There’s no way to know whether a lounge or classroom is free until you get there.

02

Wasted time

Roaming campus for a seat cuts into the time you meant to spend studying.

03

Messy spaces

Trash or spills go unreported, so the next student finds a space they can’t use.

How it works

Five tabs cover finding a spot, booking a room, getting in, and keeping spaces clean.

Map

See every study spot on the Stevens campus map, color-coded as free, in use, or needs cleaning. Tap a building for hours and features.

Reserve

Book a classroom for a 2-hour slot, filtered by outlets, whiteboards, projectors and more.

Access

Request after-hours ID card access to the building where you reserved a room.

Spots

Tap “I’m here” when you sit down so others know the spot is taken. Check-ins clear after 2 hours.

Report

Flag trash, spills, damaged furniture or other issues so facilities can respond.
